# NL2 empirical lock scope: observed cohorts versus exact decisions

This audit uses the existing published aggregate source, not reconstructed hands. The accompanying machine-readable evidence records the source snapshot and hash, 28 observed facing-bet/raise cohort rows (minimum 500), integer fold/call/raise counts, and raw-to-normalized reconciliation. These are size-bucket observations for the declared populations, not exact-board locks or measured policy by private hand. The 3-bet dataset is OOP 3-bettor versus IP caller; it cannot be applied as an observed joint distribution to B13's IP 3-bettor versus CO caller.

Of 11,131 reachable opponent decision-node occurrences across the 425 published locked experiments in the inspected source packs, 1,254 matched an action-pattern regular expression (11.27%). They are neither independent observations nor evidence that exact action frequency targets were achieved. Three small subgames had complete regex coverage only. No exact joint empirical input row was verified for all required positions, board, prior sizes, stack and SPR conditions. Scenario outputs must stay labeled, and unmatched decisions stay unlocked. ISO/LMP marginal input is not part of this denominator.

The 392 MODEL rows are abstract examples, not a registry of uniquely specified solver states. The separately reviewed history screen has 152 without matching abstract history, 140 history-only without near SPR, and 100 history plus 1%-SPR candidates. All 392 remain uncertified. These figures are source-snapshot diagnostics, not current site counters.

To certify a reproducible exact node, freeze public cards, actor and exact action amounts, pot/stack, starting ranges and combo lineage; validate equilibrium for the declared action menu. To estimate an actual player-pool lock, additionally require a legally usable sample with dates/hash, deduplicated counts and sample denominators for the same jointly defined cohort, uncertainty and minimum sample rules, and recorded residual after solving. Keep no-data nodes visibly unlocked; do not infer private-hand frequencies from showdown-only data. Full coverage cannot be derived from the retained bucket file alone.
